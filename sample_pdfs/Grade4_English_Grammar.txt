CHAPTER 2: ENGLISH GRAMMAR - NOUNS, VERBS AND SENTENCE STRUCTURE
Subject: Grade 4 English Literature

1. Nouns:
A noun is a word that names a person, place, thing, or idea. Examples include teacher, classroom, book, and happiness. Proper nouns are specific names and always start with a capital letter.

2. Verbs:
A verb is an action word that describes what the subject of a sentence is doing. Examples include run, read, write, study, and explain.

3. Subject-Verb Agreement:
A singular subject takes a singular verb, and a plural subject takes a plural verb. Example: 'The student writes clearly' vs 'The students write clearly'.

4. Reading Comprehension Passage:
Reading comprehension requires understanding the main idea of a passage, identifying supporting details, and making logical inferences based on textual evidence.
